Three directions

Hero plus the first section each, at 1440 px wide. All three run the same live wall script. Click a title to open it full size (try it at phone width too).

A. The wall picked

The page opens as the app: a window with tiles, and the headline is typed into your own tile, the one that never moves. Carbon, bone and amber only; Archivo (wide) with IBM Plex Mono.

Why it wins: the product idea is the first thing you see and touch, and it still reads as a normal hero with a clear download. Risk: the window takes the full first screen, so everything rests on the mock being good.

B. TUI

Everything in one mono (Martian Mono, condensed), lowercase, boxes drawn like a terminal UI with titles cut into the borders.

Honest note: the most "terminal", but terminal cosplay is a common dev-tool look, long reading in all-mono is tiring, and it would be harder for anyone who isn't already a terminal person.

C. Instrument

Light first, like a hardware panel: huge black type, tiles as modules with an LED that lights amber, spec line in lowercase mono.

Honest note: the boldest type and a properly designed light mode, but it moves away from the app's dark terminal world, and the side-by-side tiles get thin; the pill buttons drift toward the template look.
